## Research

### The Null Hypothesis

The Null Hypothesis is MZ's research publication. It publishes essays on the systems, mathematics, and incentives behind technology, alongside interactive explanations. The four current essays examine a neural network shader, Goodhart's law and AI benchmarks, singular value decomposition, and L₀ regularization.

- English research portal: https://www.mzfortech.com/research
