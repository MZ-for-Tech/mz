## Research

### The Null Hypothesis

The Null Hypothesis is MZ's research publication. It publishes essays on the systems, mathematics, and incentives behind technology, alongside research papers and interactive explanations. The four current essays examine a neural network shader, Goodhart's law and AI benchmarks, singular value decomposition, and L₀ regularization. Its research paper investigates statistical compression of VGG19 for BloodMNIST blood-cell classification.
